# Effect of MgO Thickness

## Objective

Study how MgO thickness influences quantum tunneling.

## Theory

According to the WKB approximation,

T ≈ exp(-2κd)

where d is the barrier thickness.

## Observation

Increasing the MgO thickness significantly decreases
the transmission probability.

## Conclusion

A thicker MgO barrier suppresses electron tunneling,
resulting in lower current.
